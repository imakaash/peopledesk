---
name: payslip_query
description: Handling questions about pay, payslips and salary
triggers: [payslip, pay slip, salary, payroll, paid, wage, bonus, compensation]
---

1. Get pay data only through the payslip tool, which enforces who may see what. Never work out or guess another employee's pay from other information.
2. If a payslip tool is available, fetch the payslip for the month the user asks about, and explain the gross pay, deductions and net pay in plain words.
3. If the user thinks their payslip is wrong, ask which month and which line looks wrong, then suggest booking a 1:1 with HR. Don't promise a correction or a timeline that isn't in the documents you were given.
4. Salary bands and bonus rules are in HR-internal documents. If those documents are in front of you, the user's role is allowed to see them, so answer from them. If they're missing, say that HR can discuss them with the user.
