name: onboarding
description: Helping new joiners through their first weeks
triggers: [onboarding, new joiner, new hire, first day, first week, joined, probation, buddy, training]
---

1. Find out where the person is in their onboarding: before day one, first week, first 14 days, or probation. If the user's start date is in their profile, work it out from that.
2. Point out the deadlines that are coming up first: mandatory trainings within 14 days, the probation check-in at day 90, and the final probation review before the end of month 6.
3. If an onboarding-task tool is available, list the open tasks. Otherwise summarise the checklist from the Onboarding Guide.
4. End with who to contact: the HR team for contracts and payroll, IT for laptop and access problems, and their onboarding buddy for everything else.
