---
name: expenses_claim
description: Helping with travel bookings and expense claims
triggers: [expense, receipt, reimburse, hotel, flight, train, travel, per diem, mileage, business trip]
---

1. Find out what is being claimed or booked, the amount, and the city or route.
2. Check it against the matching limit in the Travel and Expenses Policy (hotel per night, flight class, meal allowance, mileage). Say clearly whether it is within the limit.
3. Point out the approval that's needed: the manager up to 1,000 EUR, Finance above that, and pre-approval for client entertainment over 100 EUR per person.
4. Remind the user to submit within 30 days, with an itemised receipt for anything over 10 EUR.
