## Pay dates
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. The payroll cut-off for changes (new hires, leavers, salary changes) is the 15th of the month. Changes after the cut-off go into the next month's payroll.

## Salary bands
Base salary bands for 2026, gross per year: L1 Associate 48,000 to 58,000 EUR. L2 Professional 58,000 to 72,000 EUR. L3 Senior 72,000 to 90,000 EUR. L4 Staff 90,000 to 115,000 EUR. L5 Principal 115,000 to 145,000 EUR. Band data is confidential and must not be shared with employees outside HR.

## Bonus
The annual bonus target is 0 to 15% of base salary, depending on level and performance rating. Bonuses are paid with the March payroll. Employees who joined after 1 October are not eligible for that year's bonus.

## Payroll corrections
If an employee reports an error on their payslip, HR checks it within 3 working days. Underpayments are corrected through an off-cycle payment within 5 working days. Overpayments are recovered from the next payroll, after the employee has agreed in writing.
