## Health insurance
Nimbus Labs pays 90% of the private health insurance premium for employees and 70% for registered dependants (partner and children). You have to enrol within 30 days of your start date. After that, you can only enrol during the annual open enrolment window in November or after a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Pension
The company matches your pension contributions up to 5% of your base salary. Matching starts from your first payroll. Employer contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget
Every employee gets a learning budget of 1,500 EUR per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications related to their role. Unused budget doesn't carry over. Spending over 500 EUR on a single item needs your manager's approval first. Claim it through the expenses process.

## Wellness allowance
You get a wellness allowance of 40 EUR per month for gym memberships, sports clubs, or mental-health apps. It is paid with your monthly salary once you've uploaded proof of membership.

## Equipment
New employees get a laptop of their choice (macOS or Linux) and a one-time home-office stipend of 500 EUR for a desk, chair or monitor. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
