## Our standards
We expect everyone at Nimbus Labs to treat colleagues, candidates and customers with respect. We don't tolerate discrimination, harassment or bullying of any kind, whether based on gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation or any other characteristic.

## Reporting concerns
You can report a concern to your manager, to HR at hr@nimbuslabs.example, or anonymously through the Speak Up hotline at speakup.nimbuslabs.example. Every report is investigated within 10 working days. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a conduct violation.

## Gifts and hospitality
You can accept gifts from suppliers or customers worth up to 50 EUR. Gifts worth more must be declared to HR and may have to be returned. Never accept cash or cash equivalents.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare any side business, paid outside work, or close relationship with a supplier, candidate or direct report to HR as soon as it comes up. Declaring a conflict isn't a violation. Hiding one is.
