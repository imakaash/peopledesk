## Before your first day
Your laptop is shipped to your home address 5 working days before your start date. You get your accounts (email, Slack, HR system) within 24 hours of signing your contract. You'll be assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team, who will contact you before day one.

## First week
On your first day you meet your manager for a welcome 1:1 and set your first-week goals. In week one, finish setting up your laptop, read the engineering or team handbook, and book a 30-minute intro with your onboarding buddy.

## Mandatory training
You must complete these trainings within 14 days of your start date: Information Security Basics, Code of Conduct, and Data Protection (GDPR). Your manager is notified if training is overdue.

## Probation
Every new employee has a 6-month probation period. You'll have a probation check-in with your manager at day 90, and a final probation review before the end of month 6. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks for both sides.

## Who to ask
For questions about payroll, contracts or benefits, contact the HR team at hr@nimbuslabs.example, or book a 1:1 with HR through PeopleDesk. For laptop or access problems, contact IT at it@nimbuslabs.example.
