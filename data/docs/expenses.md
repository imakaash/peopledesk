## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemised receipt for every item over 10 EUR. Expenses approved by the 20th of the month are reimbursed with that month's salary. Later ones are paid the following month.

## Approval limits
Your direct manager approves expenses up to 1,000 EUR per claim. Claims over 1,000 EUR also need Finance approval. Client entertainment over 100 EUR per person needs manager approval before the event.

## Flights and trains
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. For trips under 4 hours by rail, take the train instead of flying.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is 180 EUR per night. In London, Paris, New York, San Francisco and Zurich the limit is 250 EUR per night. Book through the company travel portal where you can.

## Meals and per diem
When travelling for work, you can claim a meal allowance of 45 EUR per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it's part of approved client entertainment.

## Mileage
Using your own car for business trips is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre. Your commute between home and the office is not reimbursed.
