## Hybrid schedule
Nimbus Labs works hybrid. Employees are expected in the office 2 days per week, and teams choose their own office days. Fully remote contracts are only possible with HR approval and are stated in your contract.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your schedule flexibly, as long as you work your contracted hours.

## Working from abroad
You can work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. This needs approval from your manager and from HR at least 2 weeks in advance, because of tax and insurance rules. Some countries are excluded. HR will confirm when you ask.

## Home office setup
Home-office equipment is covered by the one-time 500 EUR stipend described in the Benefits Guide. You are responsible for a safe workspace and a stable internet connection.
