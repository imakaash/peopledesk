## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, on top of public holidays. Leave builds up at 2.08 days per month worked. Part-time employees get leave in proportion to their contracted hours. New joiners get a pro-rated amount for their first year based on their start date.

## Carrying over leave
You can carry up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they expire. Days above the 5-day limit expire on 31 December and are not paid out, unless your manager and HR agreed in writing that business needs stopped you from taking them.

## Sick leave
You get up to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you're sick. If you are off for more than 3 consecutive working days, you need a doctor's note. Sick leave doesn't count against annual leave, and if you get sick during annual leave you can reclaim those days with a doctor's note.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Tell HR at least 8 weeks before the leave starts.

## Requesting and approving leave
Request leave through PeopleDesk. For leave longer than 3 working days, request at least 2 weeks in advance. Your direct manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Approved leave shows in your leave balance straight away. If a request is declined, your manager has to give a reason.

## Unpaid leave and compassionate leave
After 12 months of service, you can request up to 20 days of unpaid leave per year, which needs manager and HR approval. Compassionate leave is 5 paid days when a close family member dies, and you don't need to request it in advance.
